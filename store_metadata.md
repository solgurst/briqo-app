### 5. Long Description (Max 4000 characters)
```text
Meet Briqo, a compassionate companion designed to work with your ADHD mind, never against it! 

Guided by our empathetic mascot Brix the Octopus, Briqo helps you manage daily routines, stay focused, and balance emotional waves. Unlike standard productivity apps, Briqo is specifically tailored for the dopamine needs and neuro-diverse patterns of the ADHD brain.

🌟 KEY FEATURES:

🧠 MIND PROFILE QUIZ (ONBOARDING)
Discover how your mind processes tasks. Identify as a "Wind Explorer" (inattentive type) or "Action Engine" (hyperactive type) so Brix can customize its responses and coaching style to your brain.

🫂 NO-SHAME STREAK SYSTEM
No more guilt trips! If you miss a day, your streak isn’t broken. For longer absences, Brix greets you not with red warning alerts, but with loving, empathetic, and encouraging messages.

🍹 THE DOPAMINE MENU
When you feel understimulated, pick from curated healthy micro-activities (e.g., cold water shock, 30s dance party) to boost your focus levels. Earn virtual Dopamine Points (DP) upon completion!

💔 RSD (REJECTION SENSITIVITY DYSPHORIA) SUPPORT
During periods of emotional distress or RSD triggers, toggle the "Sensitive" mood. Brix changes its expression and walks you through deep breathing exercises, self-compassion affirmations, and cozy prompts.

⏳ WAITING MODE HELPER
Stuck in "waiting mode" before an appointment, unable to start anything? Set your timer and Brix will keep track of the clock, suggesting just one small, manageable task to keep you moving safely.

🎯 HYPERFOCUS ALARM
Prevent burnout and lose track of time no more. The 45-minute friendly focus alarm gently interrupts you to hydrate, stretch, and decide if you want to extend your focus session.

🫧 ASMR FIDGET BALLOON POPPING
Calm physical restlessness with an interactive bubble-popping fidget board. Experience satisfying visual pop effects and ASMR audio feedback.

🛍️ DOPAMINE SHOP & BRIX'S WARDROBE
Spend your hard-earned Dopamine Points (DP) on cute accessories like cool sunglasses, a wizard hat, or a crown. Brix wears your purchased items across all screens in the app!

Briqo celebrates neurodiversity, providing a playful, safe space to organize your life without the pressure of typical productivity tools.

Note: This application is a screening and supportive tool, not a replacement for professional medical diagnosis or treatment.
```
